♡ Privacy Policy ♡

Last Updated: October 4, 2026
Yume Registry is a small independent project made for the yume community. This page explains what information the bot stores and how it is used. ♡

1. Information Stored

Yume Registry stores only the information voluntarily submitted for a registry entry:

  • F/O name
  • Fandom
  • Sharing status
  • Relationship

Yume Registry does not store message content, passwords, Discord authentication tokens, email addresses, or other sensitive account credentials as part of the registry.

2. How Information Is Used

Submitted information is used only to provide the registry functionality of Yume Registry.

Registry information is not sold or used for advertising.

3. User-Submitted Information

Registry information is submitted voluntarily by users. Users are responsible for the information they choose to include in their entries.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information or private information belonging to another person.

4. Data Sharing

Yume Registry does not sell submitted registry information or provide it to third parties for advertising or marketing.

Information submitted to the registry may be displayed through the bot when necessary for its intended registry functionality.

5. Data Retention

Submitted information may remain stored while the corresponding registry entry exists.

The registry is limited to the four fields described above: F/O name, fandom, sharing status, and relationship.

6. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their registry information through the project's official support or contact method.

Once an entry is deleted, its registry information will no longer be retained by Yume Registry unless retention is required for legitimate security or legal reasons.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by Yume Registry. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Discord

Yume Registry is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord.

Discord may separately process information according to its own policies when users interact with Discord or authorize an application.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the project or its features change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when revisions are made.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning registry information may be submitted through Yume Registry's official support or contact method.
